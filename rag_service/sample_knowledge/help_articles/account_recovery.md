# Account Recovery

## Lost Access To Login Email

If you lost access to your login email, first check whether another Workspace Admin can update your email address.

Admin-assisted recovery is preferred for members and team leads.

If no admin is available, contact support with:

- Workspace URL.
- Old email address.
- Requested new email address.
- Your workspace role.
- Reason you lost access.

CloudDesk will not change the email until identity verification is complete.

## Workspace Owner Or Admin Recovery

For Workspace Owner or Admin recovery, the requester must prove authority over the workspace.

Verification may include:

- Reply from the verified billing email on file.
- Confirmation from another active Workspace Owner.
- Recent invoice number and billing details.
- Enterprise contract contact confirmation.
- Domain ownership verification using a DNS TXT record.

## Verification Steps

CloudDesk may ask you to:

1. Confirm workspace URL and login email.
2. Confirm your role and last successful login date if available.
3. Confirm billing or admin relationship.
4. Verify access to the company domain or billing contact.
5. Provide additional details if answers conflict with account records.

## Enterprise Admin Recovery

Enterprise admin recovery may involve the Customer Success Manager or Enterprise Support lead.

If all admins are locked out because of SSO misconfiguration, CloudDesk will prioritize the request based on production impact.

## Security Restrictions

For workspace protection, CloudDesk Support cannot:

- Share account passwords or reset links with third parties.
- Disable MFA without verification.
- Change owner email based only on a forwarded message.
- Reveal workspace member lists to unverified requesters.
- Accept screenshots as the only proof of ownership.

Support can send reset instructions to the verified email on file, ask an active admin to complete the change, start domain verification, or escalate the request for security review.

## Suspected Account Takeover

Contact support immediately if you suspect account takeover, former employee access, ownership dispute, or unauthorized changes.

For your protection, CloudDesk verifies workspace ownership before changing account access.
